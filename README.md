 # Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption – Key Insights from Data Analysis

📌 1. EV Adoption by City (Total Electric Range):

Seattle leads with the highest electric range total (1.82 million), followed by Bellevue, Vancouver, and Redmond, indicating strong EV penetration in urban hubs.

📌 2. EV Adoption by Make:

TESLA dominates the market with over 100,000 vehicles, followed by Chevrolet, Nissan, and Ford — highlighting Tesla's leading position in the EV industry.

📌 3. EV Adoption by Model Year:

Recent model years show rapid growth:

2023: 59,769

2024: 48,995

2022: 28,861
This trend reflects increased EV production and adoption over time.

📌 4. EV Count by County:

King County has the highest count (118,688), followed by Snohomish and Pierce, showing concentrated EV activity in densely populated regions.

📌 5. Average Electric Range by Model:

KONA (258 miles) and ROADSTER (229.6 miles) lead in electric range, offering long-distance capabilities.

Models like RAV4 and E-GOLF have lower ranges (~100 miles), suitable for short-distance urban use.

📌 6. EV Distribution by Legislative District:

District 41 shows the highest EV presence with 14,538 Tesla vehicles, indicating potential regional incentives or higher EV awareness.

📌 7. Adoption by Electric Utility Providers:

Puget Sound Energy Inc (alone or in combination) supports the majority of EVs, with over 85,000 vehicles, showcasing its infrastructure strength in powering clean mobility.

📌 8. Top Census Tracts for EV Registration (2020 Data):

Tract 53033028200 tops the list with 2,647 EVs, suggesting dense clusters of environmentally conscious consumers in specific zones.

📌 9. Highest Base MSRP by EV Model:

Tesla Model S has the highest base price (~₹99.4 lakh), while models like Pacifica and Countryman cater to mid-segment buyers.

📌 10. City-wise Base MSRP:

Klickitat and Clallam Bay top the list with high average MSRPs, while Silverlake and Carbonado show preference for budget EVs, reflecting economic diversity in adoption.
